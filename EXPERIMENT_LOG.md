# Experiment Log

> Record every meaningful run, including failures. Never fill in
> measurements from estimates or memory.

## Reproducibility rules

-   Use a unique experiment ID.
-   Record the exact command, configuration, model revision, and
    environment.
-   Separate measured values from estimates.
-   Run warm-ups and repeated trials; report the statistic and
    variability.
-   Keep evaluation prompts/data fixed when comparing methods.
-   Record errors, crashes, thermal throttling, and incomplete runs.
-   Change one major variable at a time unless the design explicitly
    studies interactions.
